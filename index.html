Patmos Tourism Review Analysis

Public research interfaces for a coded corpus of visitor accounts from the Cave of the Apocalypse and the Monastery of Saint John the Theologian.

Interface 01

Analytic dashboard

Explore codes, filters, prevalence, and cross-tabulations across the review corpus.

 Interface 02

Quotable anthology

Read selected passages organized by interpretive theme and coded review ID.

 Interface 03

Reading dossiers

Examine thematic cohorts and their supporting review passages.

 Interface 04

Chapter graphs

View and export figures prepared for the chapter analysis.
The public site contains pseudonymous review IDs, quotations, and analytic codes. Original spreadsheets and direct reviewer identifiers are not distributed.
